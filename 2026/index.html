Venue & Getting There

The workshop takes place on the fifth floor of the Ralph S. O'Connor Building for Engineering and Science at Rice University.

Open in Google Maps →

  • Location

    5th floor, Ralph S. O'Connor Building for Engineering and Science, Rice University, 6100 Main St, Houston, TX 77005.

  • On campus

    The O'Connor building sits on the engineering quad. Once inside, take the elevators to the 5th floor.

  • Visitor parking

    Since the workshop is on a Saturday, most visitor lots are open and available. Look for an open lot near the O'Connor building — for example the North Lot or the Cambridge Garage, among others.
